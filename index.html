How it works

A clear pipeline for curious questions.

🧭

Plan the route

The Planner breaks a broad question into concrete research steps before the model starts writing.

🔎

Gather context

The Researcher looks for relevant ideas and keeps the answer focused on evidence, not guesswork.

🧪

Stress-test claims

The Critic and Debater look for weak spots, risks, and next steps a human reviewer should check.

📄

Share a brief

The final response is formatted as a readable research brief you can copy, discuss, or revise.
